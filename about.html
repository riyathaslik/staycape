About Staycape

Who We Are

Staycape Travel Company

Staycape Travel Company is a dedicated travel agency based in Kondotty, Kerala, committed to making travel accessible, enjoyable and stress-free for every traveller.

We specialize in crafting personalized travel experiences — from weekend getaways to once-in-a-lifetime international adventures. Our team understands that every journey is unique, and we treat every client with that same care and attention.

Whether you're looking to explore the backwaters of Alappuzha, trek through the Himalayas, or experience the culture of Japan — Staycape is your trusted companion for the entire journey.

What We Do

Comprehensive Travel Solutions

At Staycape, we provide end-to-end travel services that cover every aspect of your journey. From the first enquiry to the final check-in, we handle all the planning, booking and logistics.

Our services include flight, train and bus ticket booking, resort and hotel reservations, visa consultancy, travel insurance, and fully customized domestic and international tour packages. We also assist with overseas job hunting and health tourism.

With Staycape, you don't just book a trip — you gain a travel partner who genuinely cares about your experience.
